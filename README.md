# MoodBoard - Your Daily Visual Diary

## Introduction
MoodBoard is an open-source project that allows users to create and maintain a visual diary of their daily moods and experiences. With a sleek and intuitive interface, users can easily upload images, add captions, and organize their entries by date.

## Key Features
- **Visual Diary**: Upload and organize your daily photos, videos, and other visual content to create a personal mood board.
- **Mood Tracking**: Assign moods and emotions to your entries, allowing you to track and analyze your daily emotional state.
- **Journaling**: Add captions and notes to your visual entries, transforming your mood board into a rich, multimedia journal.
- **Calendar View**: Easily navigate through your entries using the built-in calendar view, allowing you to quickly find and revisit past entries.
- **Sharing and Collaboration**: (NEW FEATURE) Share your mood board with friends and family, or collaborate with others to create a shared visual diary.
